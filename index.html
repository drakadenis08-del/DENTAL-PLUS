Short answers to what patients ask before their first visit

Questions we hear most

We talk about your complaints and goals, examine your teeth and, if needed, take an X-ray or a digital scan. Then the doctor explains what he or she sees, suggests a treatment plan and gives you the cost before anything starts. A first consultation usually takes 30 to 40 minutes.

We use modern local anesthesia, so you should feel pressure but not pain. If you are anxious, tell us in advance: the doctor will take extra time, explain every step and can adjust the approach. Treating children is done gently and without rushing.

The prices on the website are starting prices ("from"). The exact cost depends on the case and is agreed with you after the examination, before treatment begins. You can pay by cash or card at the clinic.

Use the "Get a free consultation" button or the form on this page, or call us. We will call you back to confirm a convenient day and time. You can also write to us in a messenger.

It depends on the case. In many cases the implant is placed in one visit, then the bone needs a few months to heal before the crown is fitted. The doctor will give you an exact timeline after a 3D scan.

Professional whitening is done after a check-up and cleaning, with products and times chosen by the doctor. Some people feel temporary sensitivity, which usually passes in a day or two. If you have cavities or gum problems, we treat them first.

We recommend the first visit when the first teeth appear, and regular check-ups every six months after that. Early visits help to prevent problems and to make the dentist a normal, calm place for a child.

Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Dental Plus ("we", "us") is a dental clinic at 22 Khreshchatyk St., Kyiv. Contact for privacy questions: use the phone numbers listed on this website.

2. What data we collect

When you request a consultation or sign up, we collect the details you enter: name, surname, phone number, email, chosen service, problem description and any comment. We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

3. Health information

Information about your health that you share in the form is used only so that a doctor can prepare for your visit. Please do not include detailed medical records in the form; share them with the doctor at the appointment.

4. Why we use it

We use your data to contact you, confirm your appointment, remind you about your visit and answer your questions. We do not use it for any other purpose without your consent.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is available only to clinic staff and to service providers that help us run the website, and only as far as needed. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

6. Storage and your rights

We keep data for as long as needed to provide care and as required by law. You may ask us to show, correct or delete your data by contacting the clinic.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
